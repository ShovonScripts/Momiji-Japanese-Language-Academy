Our Story

Building Bridges Between Bangladesh and Japan

Founded in 2020, Momiji Japanese Language Academy emerged from a vision to provide authentic, disciplined Japanese language education in Dhaka. Our name 'Momiji' (紅葉) means maple leaf, symbolizing the beautiful changing of seasons and our students' transformation as they progress in their Japanese language journey.

What started as a small class of 10 students has now grown into one of Dhaka's most respected Japanese language institutes, with over 300 successful graduates and counting. Our focus remains on quality over quantity, maintaining small batch sizes for personalized attention.
