Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior knowledge?

No, our N5 course is designed for absolute beginners. We start from Hiragana and Katakana.

What textbooks do you use?

We use the popular 'Minna no Nihongo' series along with supplementary materials developed by our instructors.

Can I switch batches if needed?

Yes, subject to availability. We try to accommodate schedule changes when possible.

Do you provide certificates?

Yes, we provide course completion certificates for both N5 and N4 levels.
