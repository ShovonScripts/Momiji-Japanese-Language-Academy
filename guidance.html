Student Guidance

Ethical, honest support for your journey to Japan – from school selection to visa interview.

Our commitment

Guidance you can trust

At Momiji, we believe in transparency. We never promise visa approval – because that decision rests solely with the Embassy. What we do offer is thorough, professional preparation to give you the best possible chance.

Our guidance covers every step: selecting the right school, preparing flawless documents, financial proof guidance, interview practice, and pre‑departure orientation.

Step by step

How we support you

Clear milestones on your path to Japan.

1

Initial consultation

We discuss your goals, academic background, financial capacity, and preferred cities. We explain realistic timelines and answer all your questions – no obligation.

2

School selection

Based on your profile, we recommend accredited Japanese language schools / vocational colleges. We provide brochures, application requirements, and deadlines.

3

Document preparation

We guide you through every form: application forms, study plan, financial statement, graduation certificates. We review drafts and help you avoid common mistakes.

4

Interview coaching

Many schools/embassy conduct interviews. We hold mock interviews, provide feedback, and build your confidence – in English and Japanese.

5

Pre‑departure orientation

Once your visa is granted, we brief you on living in Japan: accommodation, part‑time jobs, cultural norms, and what to pack.

Paperwork

Typical application documents

For the school

  • Application form (school specific)
  • Study plan / statement of purpose
  • Graduation certificates (academic)
  • Academic transcripts
  • JLPT certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport copy

Financial documents

  • Bank statement (usually 6 months)
  • Sponsor's letter of guarantee
  • Proof of employment / income (sponsor)
  • Tax certificates (if available)

We review all documents before submission to ensure accuracy.

Our ethical promise

Momiji never guarantees visa approval – such promises are misleading and against our values. We provide expert preparation so you can submit the strongest possible application. Visa decisions are made solely by the Japanese Embassy.

Guidance FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge for guidance?

Initial consultation is free. If you enrol in our language course, basic guidance is included. For students who only need application support, we offer a separate fee – please contact us.

Which schools do you work with?

We have partnerships with over 20 accredited Japanese language schools in Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, Fukuoka, and more. We also assist with vocational college applications.

What if my visa is rejected?

We review the reasons with you and help strengthen your next application. We do not charge extra for re‑application guidance within the same intake.
